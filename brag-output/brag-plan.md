# Brag Plan: Samagna · ସମଗ୍ନ

## Rubric Answers

1. **What is the app?**
   Samagna (ସମଗ୍ନ) is a production-grade digital custody tracking, fair-trade ledger, and B2B marketplace for Minor Forest Produce in Odisha (TDCCOL / LABHA), directly securing livelihoods for tribal gatherers.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "100% Minimum Support Price (MSP) guaranteed — zero weight shrinkage deductions passed to tribal gatherers."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The deep Sal-forest canopy (`#12200D` to `#203a15`) with warm golden accents, rich typography (Fraunces serif display + clean sans), and live cryptographic batch provenance badges.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   - The headline: *"From the forest to the marketplace"* + Odia typography *ସମଗ୍ନ*
   - The 4-step custody chain: Village Collection -> Depôt Reweighing -> Lab Quality -> B2B Trade
   - Botanical Produce Cards: Wild Honey, Mahua Flower, Sal Seed with MSP badges
   - Real-time Traceability QR & batch status verification (`BS-402`)

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   20.0 seconds. 5 focused beats with rhythm and punch.

6. **What tone fits best?**
   - Preset: `polished`
   - Creative direction: *Prestigious, grounded, cinematic product showcase celebrating tribal economic empowerment.*

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Warm, energetic organic beat with uplifting rhythm and crisp UI tactile sound effects.

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "From the Similipal canopy to global enterprise: Samagna brings 100% MSP guarantee and end-to-end provenance to tribal forest produce in Odisha."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Village collection (digital scale gross-tare) -> Depôt custody triage -> Enterprise B2B lot purchase with instant statutory DBT settlement.

## Storyboard (20.0s Total)

### Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 4.0s)
- **Visual**: Deep forest canopy background emerges. Large warm cream typography: *"From the forest to the marketplace."* Odia crest: *ସମଗ୍ନ · ସ୍ୱଚ୍ଛ ବନଜାତ ବାଣିଜ୍ୟ*.
- **Subtext**: "Minor Forest Produce Custody Tracking & B2B Trade · Mayurbhanj, Odisha"
- **Motion**: Cinematic zoom-in, smooth typographic reveal with glowing green canopy backdrop.

### Scene 2: The Invariant (4.0s – 8.0s)
- **Visual**: 3 statutory metric cards snap into center:
  - `100% MSP DBT` (Zero gatherer deductions)
  - `17 Minor Forest Produces` (Statutory price protection)
  - `140,000+ Gatherers` (TDCCOL & LABHA statutory governance)
- **Motion**: Staggered spring entrance, card highlight glow.

### Scene 3: Botanical Produce & Real UI (8.0s – 12.5s)
- **Visual**: High-fidelity botanical produce showcase:
  - *Wild Forest Honey* (APIS-DOR-01 · ₹320/kg MSP)
  - *Mahua Flower* (MADH-IND-01 · ₹40/kg MSP)
  - *Sal Tree Seeds* (SHOR-ROB-01 · ₹28/kg MSP)
- **Motion**: Smooth carousel rail transition, verified price tags popping in.

### Scene 4: Digital Custody & Traceability QR (12.5s – 16.5s)
- **Visual**: Batch `BS-402` digital waybill & cryptographic QR code with verified journey:
  - Karanjia VPC (Collected) -> Baripada Depôt (Weighed) -> Lab Clearance (Grade A Certified).
- **Motion**: Scanning beam across the batch QR code, green verified checkmarks lighting up.

### Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action (16.5s – 20.0s)
- **Visual**: Samagna brand seal, official partner trust strip (TDCCOL · LABHA · Govt of Odisha).
- **Headline**: "Empowering Indigenous Forest Livelihoods."
- **URL**: `samagna.vercel.app`
- **Motion**: High-impact settle, final brand mark hold with subtle lens glow.
